### Voiceover Recording Tips

1. **Record in a closet** — clothes absorb echo, gives studio-quality sound
2. **Use your phone** — iPhone/Android voice memos are good enough
3. **Read each segment 3 times** — pick the best take
4. **Speak 20% slower than normal** — video compresses time
5. **Pause after numbers** — "five point nine four to one" [pause] "thrust to weight ratio"
6. **Export as WAV** — import into video editor for sync

### Music Guidelines

- **Start:** Minimal, tense, almost silent (Segment 1-2)
- **Build:** Add subtle pulse when robot appears (Segment 3)
- **Peak:** Loudest during dashboard demo (Segment 4)
- **Energy:** Fast, driving beat during swarm station (Segment 5)
- **Resolve:** Calm, confident during hardware/vision (Segment 6-7)
- **End:** Single sustained note, fade to silence (Segment 8)

Search YouTube Audio Library for: *"tension drone"*, *"cinematic minimal"*, *"tech pulse"*
