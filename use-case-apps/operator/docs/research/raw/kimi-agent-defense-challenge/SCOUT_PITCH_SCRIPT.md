## DELIVERY NOTES

### When to Pause
- After **"one hundred thirty euros"** — 1 second
- After **"four point five percent don't come back"** — 2 seconds
- After each **video clip** — let it finish, then speak
- After **"four hundred forty-nine euros"** — 1 second
- After **"twenty-five thousand ground robots"** — 1 second
- At **"Thank you"** — 5 seconds of silence

### What NOT to Do
- Don't say "um" or "uh"
- Don't look at your laptop screen while talking
- Don't read the script verbatim — memorize the key phrases
- Don't rush the numbers — pause after each one
- Don't gesture at the screen — gesture at the judges

### Body Language
- Feet shoulder-width apart
- Hands at your sides or gesturing toward the judges
- Eye contact with each judge
- Point at the screen only when showing the dashboard clicks
- End with hands at your sides, confident posture
