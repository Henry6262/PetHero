## 2. Drone Swarm — "All In One Line, Bomb Drones In The Middle"

### Where to Say It
**ACT 2 — The Vision** (while the swarm video plays)

### Why It Matters
This is your tactical insight. It's doctrinally sound (echelon formation) and operationally proven (Ukraine's Swarmer system). Shows you understand military tactics, not just tech.

### What to Say (ONE sentence)
```
"Six drones in echelon formation — recon on the wings, 
payload in the center. One operator. One screen."
```

### Deeper Context (for Q&A)
```
"Ukraine's Swarmer has executed over a hundred swarm operations 
with three to twenty-five drones. The echelon line is standard 
UAV doctrine — lead drone scouts, middle drones carry payload, 
rear drone relays comms. We do it from a ground station 
so it's immune to jamming."
```

## 3. Loitering Munitions / Ambush Mode

### Where to Say It
**ACT 2 — The Vision** (while the ambush video plays)

### Why It Matters
The Dron-Zhdun (waiting drone) is one of the most significant tactical innovations of the Ukraine war. Your ground-based version is **harder to detect** than aerial — no rotor noise, no visual signature from above, looks like debris.

### What to Say (ONE sentence)
```
"Russia's waiting drones hibernate six hours to thirty days. 
Ours does it on the ground — two watts, thirteen hours, 
completely silent."
```

### Deeper Context (for Q&A)
```
"The Dron-Zhdun lands, powers down, and waits. Fiber-optic link 
so it can't be jammed. Our ground version is even harder to detect 
— no rotor noise, no visual signature, looks like rubble. 
The IMX500 AI watches. When something moves, the mesh network 
wakes up and alerts the operator."
```
