# Krava - Doesn't Fall

**Goal:** 15-second vertical conversion ad for Zurich premium-food buyers.

**Hook:** The Spoon Test. A spoon stands straight up in thick Bulgarian yogurt.

**VO:**

> This spoon doesn't fall. Four thousand years of Bulgarian craft. Living cultures. Nothing added. Made fresh in Zurich. Krava: Swiss purity, Bulgarian soul. Order today.

| Time | Visual | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 5-10s | Creamy yogurt folds slowly, honey and berries hit the surface | 4,000 YEARS. NOTHING ADDED. |
| 10-15s | Premium breakfast counter, jar/bowl, berries, clean Zurich apartment light | FRESH IN ZURICH. ORDER TODAY. |

## Conversion Logic

- **Proof first:** spoon standing upright implies thickness without explanation.
- **Authority:** Bulgarian yogurt heritage and living cultures.
- **Local relevance:** made fresh in Zurich.
- **CTA:** direct order prompt, no vague brand ending.
