# Krava — The Flip Challenge (phone-filmable storyboard)

**The idea:** AI can't render the gravity-flip (we tried — yogurt renders as liquid). But
*real* thick yogurt can't fail the physics. So the hero shot is filmed by a human on a
phone, and we cut the **AI knife-slice** in as the payoff. Real stakes (yogurt over your
head) = people watch to the end.

**Format:** vertical 9:16, ~15s (organic) / ~18s (paid). One person + one jar + a phone.

---

## ⚠️ Do this FIRST (5-min product test, off camera)
The whole ad dies if the yogurt sags. Before filming:
1. Use your **thickest, cold, strained** batch (cold = firmer; pull it straight from the fridge).
2. Stand a spoon in it — it must stay upright on its own.
3. **Test-flip the jar over a sink/towel.** Hold fully inverted ~2 seconds. If nothing
   moves → you're good. If it sags → strain more / colder / shorten the on-camera hold to ~1s.
4. Film over a towel anyway for the first real take. Confidence beats a wet shirt.

## 📱 Gear & setup
- Phone vertical, **4K 60fps** if available (60 lets you slow the flip slightly in edit).
- **Side window light**, never harsh overhead. Clean white/marble surface, plain background.
- iPhone: tap-and-hold to **lock focus + exposure** (AE/AF Lock) so it doesn't hunt mid-flip.
- Tripod or steady hand for the talking bits; the **flip can be handheld** — a little shake adds "real."

## ✂️ Editing notes
- **Hard cuts on the beat**, no fades/transitions. Energy lives in the cut.
- **Organic:** mute your VO, lay a **trending audio** track, keep only the captions. Native > polished.
- **Paid:** keep your VO, add a quiet music bed, end on the CTA + real jar.
- Captions: bold white, bottom third (match the look of the existing cuts).
- The AI knife clip is **5s** — use ~2-3s of it, on its strongest frame (the wedge lifting).
- Keep the over-head hold (shot 3) to **~2s max** — long enough for tension, short enough to be safe.

## 🔁 If a take fails
- Yogurt sags on the flip → colder/thicker product, shorten the hold, or shoot the flip
  at a 90° tilt instead of full 180° (still reads as "it holds").
- You laugh/break → keep it. Bloopers of a *successful* flip ("I genuinely didn't trust it") are gold; post as a follow-up.
