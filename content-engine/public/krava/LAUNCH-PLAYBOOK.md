# Krava — Launch Playbook (Zürich DTC)

Honest, no-hype plan to turn this ad library into orders. Numbers are **Swiss-market
estimates**, not promises. Golden rule: **organic first (free), paid only to scale what
already works.** Cold paid for a brand-new premium food brand rarely profits on the first
order — you profit on repeat + subscription LTV + the retargeting pool you build.

---

## The 3-week plan (TL;DR)
1. **Week 1 — Organic, CHF 0.** Post the viral cuts daily. Find what pops for free.
2. **Week 2 — Paid test, ~CHF 350–500.** Test 3–4 creatives, find the winner.
3. **Week 3 — Scale + retarget.** 70% budget on the winner, cheap retargeting set.

---

## Phase 0 — Organic first (FREE, do this now)
Post natively, vertical, **trending audio**, mute the VO where it helps. Caption hook on frame 1.

| Day | Post | Platform | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | `08-baby-legend` (god-baby) | TikTok + Reels | Meme swing — highest viral ceiling |
| Tue | `01-thickness-test` | TikTok + Reels | Strong proof hook |
| Wed | `07-creator-muse` | IG Reels | Premium, aspirational |
| Thu | a `06-heritage` / Legends cut | Reels | Story / brand |

- Post **1–2x/day**, consistently > sporadically. Algorithm rewards cadence.
- Reply to every comment in hour 1. Re-post winners as variants.
- **Hashtags:** mix broad + niche: #zürich #zurich #bulgarianyogurt #kiselomляko #thickyogurt #swissmade #breakfasttok #guthealth (skip medical claims).
- Put a **shop link in bio** + a pinned "where to buy" comment. Track with a UTM.

## Phase 1 — Paid creative test (~CHF 350–500)
**Goal: find the winning ANGLE, not to profit yet.** Don't spread thin.

- **Structure:** 1 CBO campaign, **CHF 50–80/day**, **3–4 creatives**, run **5–7 days**.
- **Per creative you need ~CHF 100–150** to trust the signal.
- **Platforms:** TikTok first (cheaper CPM in CH), then mirror the winner to Meta Reels.
- **Test these 4 angles** (one creative each, EN first, Zürich + 30km, age 25–45):
  1. `01-thickness-test` (proof)
  2. `04-fresh-delivery` (convenience)
  3. `07-creator-muse` (premium/UGC)
  4. `08-baby-legend` (viral/meme)
- **CTA:** cold = "Try your first jar — fresh Zürich delivery." (no discount until OPD 30-day rule met).

### Kill / scale rules (check at 48h, then day 5)
- **Hook rate (3-sec view) < 25%** → creative is weak, kill it.
- **CTR < 0.8%** → kill. **CTR > 1.5%** → promising, keep.
- **CPC > CHF 2** with no add-to-carts → kill.
- **Cost per purchase > 3× your jar margin and not improving** → pause, rework offer/LP.
- Winner = best **cost per add-to-cart / purchase**, not most likes.

## Phase 2 — Scale + retarget
- Move **70% of budget to the single winner**; make 3 fresh variants of *its* angle.
- Launch a **retargeting** ad set (video-viewers + site-visitors): use a `05-living-cultures`
  or `03-morning-ritual` cut + the subscription CTA "Keep your fridge stocked — subscribe weekly."
- Only here introduce **"−20% first delivery"** — and ONLY if the base price ran 30 days first (Swiss OPD).

## Benchmarks (Swiss, estimates)
| Metric | Meta (CH) | TikTok (CH) |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | CHF 12–25 | CHF 5–12 |
| CTR (good creative) | 1–2% | 1–2.5% |
| CPC | CHF 0.60–1.50 | CHF 0.30–0.90 |
| Cold landing→purchase | 1–3% | 1–2% |

**What ~CHF 500 (TikTok-leaning) realistically returns:** ~50–60k views → ~700 clicks →
**~10–15 first orders.** At ~CHF 30 starter-bundle AOV ≈ CHF 350–450 back = roughly
break-even/slight loss on first order. Profit comes later from repeat + subscription.

## Budget tiers
- **Lean (CHF ~250):** CHF 40/day CBO, 3 creatives, 6 days. Enough to spot a clear winner.
- **Standard (CHF ~500):** CHF 70/day CBO, 4 creatives, 7 days. Reliable read + early retargeting pool. ← recommended
- **Aggressive (CHF ~1,200):** CHF 120/day, 5–6 creatives, 10 days + retargeting set. Faster learning, builds audiences fast.

## Before you spend a franc — setup checklist
- [ ] Shopify/store live with a clean **single-jar + starter-bundle** product page.
- [ ] **Meta Pixel + TikTok Pixel** installed; Purchase + AddToCart events firing.
- [ ] **UTMs** on every link (so organic vs paid is attributable).
- [ ] Real **Krava jar** composited onto every ad's end frame (AI vessels are unbranded).
- [ ] Landing page **headline mirrors the ad** (e.g. spoon-test ad → spoon-test LP).
- [ ] Delivery promise on the page matches any "48h / Mon→Wed" ad claim.

## Compliance gate (carry from AD-MATRIX.md)
No medical/gut/digestion claims · no specific culture count until verified · no
raw/unpasteurised/zero-sugar/high-protein unproven · no "−20%" badge until 30-day base
price (OPD) · Swiss/Bio marks only if certified · "strongman/legend/grows big" = obvious
comedy, keep it clearly tongue-in-cheek (not a literal health claim).
