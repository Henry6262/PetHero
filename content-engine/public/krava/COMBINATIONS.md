## CapCut quick-tips
- **Trim each clip to 2–3.5s.** 5s each is too slow; cut on the action.
- **Hard cuts** on the beat of the audio. Skip fancy transitions.
- **Captions:** big, bold, bottom third, 1 line at a time (match the look of the finished ads in `01–09`).
- **Speed ramps** on the ASMR/texture clips (slow the satisfying moment).
- **End card:** real jar + lockup, 1.5–2s, with the CTA.
- Make a **9:16 master** + export a **1:1** crop for feed.
