# Krava — Viral Clip & Ad Ideas

Rule of thumb: **organic IG/TikTok = pattern interrupt + native feel.** Paid ads = same
hook, but with a clear CTA bolted on at the end. The hook earns the view; the offer earns
the click. AI shoots the impossible-physics money shots; your team adds the human + real jar.

Legend: 🤖 = AI-generatable now (Kling) · 🎥 = needs real filming/UGC · 🧊 = real jar composite

---

## A. The "Thickness" universe (our strongest, ownable lane)
Krava's one undeniable, demonstrable truth is **density**. Own it like Coconut Cult owns
"thick." Every one of these is a 3-second wordless hook.

1. **The Upside-Down Jar** 🤖 — flip the jar 180°, nothing falls. *(building this now.)*
2. **The Knife Cut** 🤖 — slice a clean wedge like cheesecake; the edge holds. *(building.)*
3. **The Standing Peak** 🤖 — ribbon drops from spoon, stands like soft-serve. *(building.)*
4. **The Spoon Walk** 🎥 — stand a spoon in the jar, carry it across the kitchen, spoon never tips.
5. **The Flip Challenge** 🎥 — "we flip the jar over our own head. trust issues?" Real founder, real stakes = comedy + proof.
6. **Thick vs Thin split-screen** 🤖🎥 — supermarket yogurt pours like water (left) ‖ Krava holds on an upside-down spoon (right). Comparison = high retention.
7. **The Drop Test** 🤖 — dollop dropped from height onto a plate, holds its shape, doesn't splat.
8. **Stack-a-Tower** 🎥 — stack 5 scoops into a leaning tower; "how many before it falls?"
9. **Straw test** 🎥 — try (and fail) to drink it through a straw. "it's not a drink. it's a meal."

## C. Sensory / ASMR (sound-led, zero VO — let the texture talk)

18. **Honey-on-thick** 🤖 — golden honey pools on the surface and sits (doesn't sink fast = thick).
19. **The Drag** 🤖 — spoon drags a clean canyon through the surface; walls stay sharp.

## D. Story / brand (middle-funnel, builds trust & price justification)
21. **2,000 km, 1 recipe** 🤖🎥 — Bulgarian mountains → Zürich kitchen, one continuous-feel cut.
22. **"What my grandma taught me about milk and time."** 🎥 — founder voice, slow craft B-roll.

## E. Paid-ad versions (hook from A/B + CTA tail)
Every winner above becomes an ad by adding a 2-3s CTA card:
- Cold/prospecting CTA: **"Try your first jar — fresh Zürich delivery."** (low pressure, Swiss-safe)
- Retargeting CTA: **"Keep the fridge stocked — weekly delivery."**
- Offer CTA (⚠ only after 30-day base-price rule, see compliance): **"−20% your first delivery."**
- Always end on the real 🧊 jar + "Swiss Purity, Bulgarian Soul" lockup for brand recall.

## What makes these actually pop (production checklist)
- **Hook in 0.0–1.5s.** The impossible visual or the bold text must hit before frame 45.
- **Native > polished** for organic. Shaky real hand can beat perfect AI for reach. Use the AI money shot as the *payoff*, mid-clip, not the whole video.
- **Trending audio** on organic posts (AI VO is for paid). Leave room — many organic cuts need NO voiceover.
- **One idea per clip.** Don't stack 3 claims. Thick OR fresh OR heritage — pick one.
- **Real jar every time.** Composite 🧊 the actual Krava jar/label on the end frame; never let AI hallucinate the packaging.
- **Series > one-offs.** #6 "rating yogurts" and #13 can run weekly — algorithm rewards consistency.

## Compliance guardrails (carry over from AD-MATRIX.md)
- No gut/digestion/immunity/medical claims. "Living cultures" = descriptor only.
- No specific culture count until lab-verified. No raw/unpasteurised/zero-sugar/high-protein unproven.
- "−20%" badge only after the base price ran 30 consecutive days (Swiss OPD 2025).
- "Swiss / Bio / no-pesticide" trust marks only if actually certified (Swissness rules).
